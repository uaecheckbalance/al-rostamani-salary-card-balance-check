# How to Check an Al Rostamani Salary Card Balance Online

By Daniel Collins

An Al Rostamani salary card is a payroll card, so the balance you see should change when salary is credited and when you spend, withdraw cash, or pay a fee. Checking it online is usually quicker than visiting a branch, and it gives you a record to compare against your payslip. This guide explains the portal steps, the details you need, how to read the result, and what to do if the balance looks wrong.

## What You Need Before You Start

- Your Al Rostamani salary card number, or the card itself if the portal asks for part of the number for verification.
- The mobile number or email address linked to the card, because a verification code may be sent there.
- Your date of birth or another identity detail, if the portal asks for it.
- A secure device and connection. Avoid public Wi-Fi for balance checks when you can, and never enter card details on a page reached from an unexpected message.

## Step 1: Open the Correct Portal

1. Use the official Al Rostamani portal or app link provided by your employer or printed on the card materials.
2. Check the web address carefully before you sign in, and look for the secure connection indicator in your browser.
3. Do not use a search result that looks like an advertisement if you are unsure which link is official. Go back to the link your employer gave you.

## Step 2: Sign In to Your Salary Card Account

1. Enter your username, card number, or registered mobile number, exactly as the portal requests.
2. Enter your password or PIN in the field provided. Take care with similar characters and with Caps Lock.
3. Enter the one time code sent to your registered phone or email, if the portal asks for one.
4. If you have forgotten your password, use the official reset option on the portal rather than sharing details with anyone by phone or chat.

## Step 3: Find and Read Your Balance

1. After sign in, open the salary card or account summary page.
2. Look for the available balance first. This is normally the amount you can spend or withdraw.
3. Compare it with the current or total balance if both are shown. A difference can mean a pending transaction or a hold.
4. Open the recent transactions list and check the date, description, and amount for salary credits, purchases, ATM withdrawals, and fees.

## How to Read the Result

A salary credit should appear shortly after payday, but the exact timing depends on your employer and the card provider. A purchase may show as pending for a short time before it becomes final. An ATM withdrawal usually reduces the available balance at once. If a transaction appears twice, check whether one entry is pending and one is final before you treat it as a duplicate charge.

## What to Do If the Balance Looks Wrong

1. Refresh the page and sign out and back in, to rule out a stale display.
2. Check the transaction list for a salary credit, a fee, or a purchase you may have forgotten.
3. Compare the online balance with your latest payslip and with any receipt for a recent withdrawal.
4. Note the date, amount, and description of any transaction you dispute, and take a screenshot for your records if the portal allows it.
5. Contact the card provider or your employer payroll team through an official number, and give them the transaction details rather than your full card number or password.
6. If the card is lost or you see a transaction you did not make, block the card through the portal or official helpline at once and ask for the next steps in writing.

## Common Problems and Fixes

If the portal will not load, try a different browser, clear the cache for that site, or switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data. If login fails repeatedly, reset the password once through the official route and wait for the confirmation message before trying again. If the balance does not update after payday, allow a short processing period, then check with payroll to confirm the payment date and the account details they hold.

## Keeping Your Salary Card Safe

Sign out when you finish, especially on a shared device. Keep your password and verification codes private, review transactions each payday, and keep card statements or screenshots in a safe place. A quick weekly check makes it much easier to spot an error early and to explain it clearly to support.
